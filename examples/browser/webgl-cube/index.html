cube.lisp → WebGL

Hello 3D: this cube is drawn by a Lisp program compiled to WebAssembly. The perspective projection and the rotation matrices are computed in Lisp every frame (4×4 matrix multiplication and all) and cross into WebGL through rontolisp:wasm-import host functions — one line of JavaScript each. Even sin and cos are borrowed from JavaScript's Math.
